Electrolyte Dosing

Mix target Sodium (Na+), Potassium (K+), and Magnesium (Mg2+) based on your weight and sweat rate profile.

Osmotic Balance

Sip steadily throughout training. Avoid chugging plain water in high volume to prevent hyponatremia and flushing.
