PILLARS OF RESPIRATORY MASTERY

1. Diaphragmatic Priming

Engage the primary respiratory muscle rather than superficial chest breathing. Expands lower lung lobes for superior gas exchange efficiency.

2. Parasympathetic Shift

Extended exhales stimulate the vagus nerve, slowing heart rate variability (HRV) and dropping acute sympathetic cortisol spikes instantly.

3. Nasal Nitric Oxide

Nasal inhalation releases nitric oxide, expanding blood vessels, filtering pathogens, and increasing cellular oxygen delivery by up to 18%.
