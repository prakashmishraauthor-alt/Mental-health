Strength & Hypertrophy Splits

Structural Training
Upper Body // Biomechanical Focus

Push / Pull Mechanics

Prioritizing horizontal/vertical compound patterns with controlled eccentrics for maximum mechanical tension.

  • Incline Dumbbell Press (3x 6-8)
  • Chest-Supported Rows (3x 8-10)
  • Overhead Pressing (3x 6-8)
  • Cable Lateral Raises (4x 12-15)
Lower Body // Posterior Chain

Posterior & Quad Drive

Building structural knee resilience and hip hinge capacity while keeping spinal load managed.

  • Barbell Romanian Deadlifts (3x 6-8)
  • Hack Squats / Heel-Elevated Squats (3x 8-10)
  • Walking Lunge Extensions (3x 12/leg)
  • Seated Hamstring Curls (4x 10-12)
Mobility // Joint Health

Prehab & Joint Priming

Rotator cuff stabilization, thoracic spine extension, and ankle mobility to preserve long-term joint health.

  • Face Pulls & External Rotations (3x 15)
  • Jefferson Curls (3x 8 slow reps)
  • 90/90 Hip Mobility Flow (5 min)
  • Cossack Squat Extensions (3x 10/side)
