Core & Advanced Protocols

Methodology
🏃‍♂️

Zone 2 Running

Low-intensity steady state (LISS) training focusing on nose-breathing pace to maximize cellular lipid utilization.

🚴‍♂️

Cadence Cycling

High-rpm, smooth-pedaling indoor or outdoor intervals to build cardiovascular volume without heavy joint impact.

🏊‍♂️

Rhythmic Swimming

Full-body active recovery emphasizing breath control, core stabilization, and systemic oxygen distribution.

⚡

HIIT Tabata Sweeps

8 rounds of 20-second maximal effort sprint intervals followed by 10 seconds of rest. Designed for rapid anaerobic threshold adaptation.

🛷

Sled Pushes & Carries

Heavy concentric-only conditioning paired with farmer carries. Builds leg drive and cardiovascular toughness with zero eccentric muscle damage.

🚣‍♂️

Rowing Pyramids

Structured ergometer intervals (250m, 500m, 750m, 500m, 250m) targeting posterior chain power and stroke efficiency under fatigue.

🎒

Rucking Base Building

Weighted pack walking at a 15 min/mile pace. Builds Zone 2 endurance, posture endurance, and bone density simultaneously.
