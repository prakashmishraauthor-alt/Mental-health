MY MOVEMENT

Movement is not merely physical exercise; it is neurological medicine. When failure strikes, mental darkness takes over, or stress peaks, sitting still amplifies depression and accelerates metabolic decay. Movement shifts your physiology instantly—flushing cortisol, priming dopamine, and preserving youth.

The Rest Trap & Accelerated Aging

Resting excessively during stress or aging is a silent trap. Prolonged inactivity leads to muscle loss, joint calcification, systemic inflammation, and metabolic diseases. Motion is life; stillness under chronic stress is decay.

THE 4 NON-NEGOTIABLE MOVEMENTS FOR VITALITY
01 // SPINAL WAVE

Spinal Decompression

Gently wave the spine in cat-cow or standing rolls to re-hydrate intervertebral discs and release central nervous system tension.

Restores CNS fluid flow & posture.
02 // DEAD HANG

Bar Suspension

Hang passively from a pull-up bar for 60 seconds. Opens shoulder capsules, decompresses vertebrae, and boosts grip strength.

Key predictor of longevity.
03 // PRIMAL CRAWL

Quadrupedal Locomotion

Crawl on hands and feet across the floor. Re-wires contralateral neurological cross-patterning and builds shoulder resilience.

Re-engages deep brain-body links.
04 // DEEP SQUAT HOLD

Ground Transition

Spend 5 minutes accumulated daily in a full deep squat. Mobilizes ankles, hips, and lower back to prevent age-related mobility loss.

Protects mobility into old age.
