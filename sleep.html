Evening Wind-Down & Sleep Pack

Rest Architecture
9:00 PM — Digital Sunset
✓

Blue-Light Blocking

Disconnect screens or switch devices to night mode. Suppress high-frequency blue spectrum to trigger natural pineal melatonin release.

9:30 PM — Hydro-Cooling
✓

Warm Shower / Thermal Drop

Take a warm shower. As body heat dissipates afterward, core body temperature drops—signaling sleep readiness to the brain.

10:00 PM — Cognitive Unload
✓

Brain Dump Journaling

Write down tomorrow's top tasks or nagging thoughts. Clearing mental working memory reduces nocturnal anxiety and overthinking.

10:15 PM — Somatic Release
✓

4-7-8 Parasympathetic Breathing

Inhale 4s, hold 7s, exhale 8s for 4 cycles. Activates the vagus nerve to slow heart rate and prepare for deep slow-wave sleep.

10:30 PM — Dark Sanctuary
✓

Environment Alignment

Set ambient temperature to ~18°C (65°F), eliminate ambient LED light leaks, and enable blackout shades.

10:40 PM — Gratitude Drift
✓

Peaceful Reflection

Silently recall 3 peaceful moments from the day, allowing the nervous system to settle into restful equilibrium.
